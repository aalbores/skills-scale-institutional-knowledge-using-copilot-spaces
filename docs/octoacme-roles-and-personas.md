# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality / QA Leads

### Role Summary
Quality / QA Leads establish the test strategy and help the team verify that work meets acceptance criteria and release standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test coverage expectations and coordinate test planning
- Validate acceptance criteria with Product Managers and Developers
- Lead defect triage and track quality risks through release
- Coordinate manual QA and end-to-end smoke testing where needed
- Confirm quality evidence is ready for release decisions

### Goals
- Detect defects early and reduce escaped issues
- Provide consistent, transparent quality signals
- Support reliable releases without slowing iterative delivery

### Typical Communication
- Participate in planning, refinement, demos, and release readiness reviews
- Share test results, defect trends, and quality risks with Project Managers
- Collaborate with Developers on testability, automation, and defect resolution

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
Quality / QA Leads translate Product Managers' acceptance criteria into validation plans, work with Developers to test changes and resolve defects, and provide Project Managers with quality status and release risks. They help Stakeholders understand whether delivered work satisfies agreed outcomes.

---

## Technical Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Architects guide solution design and help teams make sustainable technical decisions across services, integrations, and major changes.

### Responsibilities
- Define and review architecture for significant features and integrations
- Document technical decisions, constraints, and trade-offs
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and migration needs
- Promote consistency in reliability, observability, and maintainability
- Support Developers during design and implementation reviews

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that are secure, scalable, and maintainable
- Reduce rework caused by unclear technical direction
- Make technical trade-offs visible to decision-makers

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews with Developers and Product Managers
- Dependency and risk discussions with Project Managers
- Architecture decision records and stakeholder briefings for significant trade-offs

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
Technical Architects partner with Developers to shape implementable designs, advise Product Managers on feasibility and trade-offs, and help Project Managers account for technical dependencies and risk in plans. They explain architectural impacts to Stakeholders without replacing the Product Manager's prioritization authority.

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Partners ensure that project decisions address applicable security, privacy, regulatory, and policy requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and compliance requirements early
- Review designs and planned releases for security risks
- Recommend mitigations and track remediation ownership
- Coordinate security testing, approvals, and incident escalation when required
- Maintain evidence needed for audits or governance reviews

### Goals
- Reduce preventable security and compliance risk
- Make security requirements actionable during planning and delivery
- Avoid late release surprises caused by missed controls

### Typical Communication
- Participate in initiation, planning, design reviews, and release readiness checks
- Record findings and mitigations in the risk register with Project Managers
- Advise Product Managers and Stakeholders on risk-based decisions
- Coordinate with Developers and Technical Architects on implementation controls

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
Security / Compliance Partners work with Product Managers to clarify policy and privacy requirements, with Technical Architects and Developers to design and implement controls, and with Project Managers to track findings, owners, and deadlines. They provide Stakeholders with risk and approval status while preserving clear accountability for remediation.

---

## Customer Advocates / UX Researchers

### Role Summary
Customer Advocates / UX Researchers represent user needs and validate that proposed solutions are understandable, usable, and valuable.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and synthesize user feedback and research findings
- Validate problem statements and proposed workflows with customers
- Identify usability, accessibility, and adoption risks
- Share evidence that informs acceptance criteria and prioritization
- Evaluate outcomes after release and recommend improvements

### Goals
- Improve customer value, usability, and adoption
- Reduce delivery risk from unvalidated assumptions
- Ensure diverse user needs are reflected in decisions

### Typical Communication
- Research readouts with Product Managers and Stakeholders
- Design and acceptance discussions with Developers and Quality / QA Leads
- Planning and milestone updates with Project Managers
- Demos and usability reviews throughout the project lifecycle

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
Customer Advocates / UX Researchers help Product Managers define outcomes and acceptance criteria using customer evidence. They collaborate with Developers and Quality / QA Leads to identify usability and accessibility scenarios, and with Project Managers to schedule research and incorporate findings into milestones. They keep Stakeholders connected to user impact without taking over product prioritization decisions.

---

## Delivery Leads / Team Facilitators

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads / Team Facilitators help the delivery team maintain flow, resolve coordination issues, and keep execution aligned with the agreed plan.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives as appropriate
- Track team-level blockers, dependencies, and follow-up actions
- Help maintain a healthy workflow and realistic capacity commitments
- Surface delivery risks and decision needs early
- Encourage continuous improvement and clear working agreements

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and team focus
- Reduce time lost to unresolved blockers and handoff gaps
- Turn retrospectives into measurable improvements

### Typical Communication
- Daily coordination with Developers and Quality / QA Leads
- Regular risk, dependency, and milestone updates with Project Managers
- Planning and priority clarification with Product Managers
- Escalations and status summaries for Stakeholders when needed

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
Delivery Leads / Team Facilitators support Project Managers by providing accurate execution signals without owning the project plan or stakeholder accountability. They help Product Managers and Developers clarify priorities and capacity, coordinate with Quality / QA Leads on readiness, and escalate blockers through the agreed path. The role enables the team rather than replacing the decision rights of Product or Project Managers.
